The Story

Where traditional joinery meets modern comfort.

Hand-shaped walnut, built to last generations.

Every Ember chair starts as a single slab of sustainably harvested American walnut. Our artisans hand-select each piece for grain pattern and structural integrity, then shape it using traditional mortise-and-tenon joinery — no screws, no brackets, just wood and craft.

The frame is finished with a hand-rubbed oil that deepens the natural warmth of the wood while protecting it from daily use. Over time, the walnut develops a rich patina that makes each chair uniquely yours.

Bouclé upholstery chosen for living, not just looking.

We source our bouclé from a family-run mill in Belgium that has been weaving premium upholstery fabrics since 1948. The textured weave is soft to the touch, remarkably durable, and naturally resistant to pilling.

The cushion core uses high-resilience foam wrapped in a down-and-feather blend, giving you that sink-in feeling without losing support. Removable covers make cleaning simple — because real life happens in real chairs.
