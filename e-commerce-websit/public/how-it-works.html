Our Process

From first look to your front door — every step is intentional.

We designed our process to remove the stress from furniture shopping. No guesswork, no hidden costs, no surprises on delivery day.

01

Tell us about your space

Share photos, measurements, and your vision. Whether it's a single room or a full home, our advisors start by listening.

02

Get personalized recommendations

Your advisor curates a selection tailored to your taste, layout, and budget. Swap finishes, adjust dimensions, or explore alternatives — it's collaborative.

03

Review your proposal

Receive a detailed proposal with 3D renders, material samples, and transparent pricing. No pressure — take your time.

04

We craft your pieces

Once confirmed, your order goes straight to our artisan workshop. Expect regular updates and photos as your furniture takes shape.

05

White-glove delivery & setup

Our delivery team brings everything to your room of choice, assembles it on site, and cleans up before leaving. All you do is enjoy.

Why our customers love the process

We handle the details so you can focus on the excitement of a new home.

Transparent timelines

Know exactly when your furniture arrives. Most pieces ship within 4–6 weeks.

Real human support

Your dedicated advisor is available by email, phone, or chat throughout the entire journey.

Satisfaction guaranteed

If something isn't right, we'll make it right — repair, replace, or return.
